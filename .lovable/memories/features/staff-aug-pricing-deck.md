# Staff Augmentation Pricing Presentation

## Overview
A standalone 7-slide pricing deck for prospects who already know the company (post-meeting). Follows a "dessert-like" philosophy: irresistible, simple, minimal cognitive load.

## Slide Sequence
1. **Cover** — Hero with "7-stage vetted talent" and "Hire in 20 minutes" hooks
2. **Pricing Tiers** — 4-column card layout (Tier 1, 2, 3.A, 3.B) with badges
3. **Feature Comparison** — 12-row matrix across all 4 tiers
4. **Cost Savings** — Animated bar charts vs US market rates (up to 76% savings)
5. **What's Included** — 6-card value proposition grid (7-stage vetting, 20-min hiring, etc.)
6. **How It Works** — 4-step timeline with 20-minute hiring badge
7. **CTA** — "Build Your Dream Team" contact slide with QR code

## Key Messaging Hooks (from ICP data)
- **7-stage vetting process** — top 5% pass rate
- **Hire in as fast as 20 minutes** — bench-ready talent
- **Up to 76% cost savings** vs US market rates

## Design
- Dark theme with accent color highlights
- Ubuntu + Poppins font pairing
- Framer Motion animations throughout
- 1920×1080 fixed aspect ratio via ScaledSlide wrapper
