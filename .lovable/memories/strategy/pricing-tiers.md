# Pricing Tiers — 4-Tier Staff Augmentation Model

## Tier 1 — Starter ($1,900/mo)
- **Experience**: 1+ year industry, 3+ years coding
- **Hours**: 160 hrs/month
- **Profile**: Tech-stack specific developer needing task guidance from Tier 2 lead
- **Best for**: Well-defined, scoped tasks
- **Includes**: Weekly progress reports

## Tier 2 — Growth ($4,299/mo) ⭐ MOST POPULAR
- **Experience**: 2-3+ years
- **Hours**: 160 hrs/month
- **Profile**: Fluent, autonomous coder who breaks down & delegates tasks
- **Best for**: Teams needing a self-sufficient developer
- **Includes**: Daily standups, sprint participation, priority Slack access

## Tier 3.A — Skilled ($6,800–$8,000/mo) 🏆 BEST VALUE
- **Experience**: 7-15+ years
- **Hours**: 160 hrs/month
- **Profile**: Best-in-class developer with deep stack expertise
- **Manages**: 3-5 team members
- **Best for**: Clients wanting top talent who can also lead a small team
- **Includes**: Architecture decisions, code quality oversight

## Tier 3.B — Leadership ($7,500–$10,000/mo) — PRICING ANCHOR
- **Experience**: 7-15+ years
- **Hours**: 160 hrs/month
- **Profile**: Project management focus, CTO-level strategic oversight
- **Manages**: Up to 15 developers
- **Best for**: Large teams needing full management layer
- **Includes**: 24/7 SLA, priority support, project management

## Pricing Psychology
- Tier 3.B serves as a **pricing anchor** — its higher price makes Tier 3.A feel like a great deal
- "Best Value" badge on 3.A guides clients toward the sweet spot
- "Most Popular" badge on Tier 2 provides social proof for mid-range buyers
- Split of Tier 3 into A/B addresses CEO feedback: clients don't want to pay for managing 15 devs when they need 1 expert coder
