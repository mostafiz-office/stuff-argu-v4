## Overview
RiseUp Asia targets 3 primary ICPs for its services. The staff augmentation pricing presentation is primarily aimed at ICP 1 and ICP 2.

## ICP 1: Early-Stage AI/DeepTech Startups
- Ex-FAANG founders, $500K–$5M funding, building MVPs
- Pain: Can't afford $180K+/yr US engineers, need niche skills fast
- Key hook: "Hire pre-vetted talent in 20 minutes"
- Equity partnership option: 30-50% salary discount for 0.5-2% equity
- Tier 3 ($8K/mo) is the pricing anchor for this segment

## ICP 2: IT Staffing Agencies (C2C Model)
- 50-200 employees, $10M-$50M revenue, Fortune 500 placements
- Pain: Vetting costs eat 20% margins, 100+ hours per hire
- Markup model: Agency adds 10% on top of RiseUp rates
- 7-stage vetting process is a key differentiator
- Mid tier at $2,600, Senior at $8,000 for C2C pricing

## ICP 3: E-commerce/DTC Brands (Digital Marketing)
- $1M-$10M revenue, Shopify/BigCommerce
- Not primary target for staff aug pricing deck

## Pricing Tiers (Staff Augmentation)
| Tier | Experience | Price/Mo | Fit |
|------|-----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | 1+ year | $1,900 | Junior coders (5-10x value) |
| 2 | 2-3 years | $4,299 | Mid-level leads (2-3x value) |
| 3 | 7-12+ yrs | $8,000 | AI/ML specialists (1x value) |

## Sales Psychology Triggers
- Startups: Fear of missing deadlines
- Agencies: Margin protection ("Save 100+ vetting hours per hire")
- Speed: 20-minute hire or 30 days for niche roles
