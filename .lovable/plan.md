
## Rocket Animation on Pricing Tier Cards (Slide 2)

### Concept
Add an animated rocket icon to each pricing tier card that visually communicates the **growth trajectory** — the higher the tier, the more dramatic the rocket animation.

### How it works

**Each card gets a small animated rocket** that launches upward on load, with increasing intensity per tier:

| Tier | Animation Style |
|------|----------------|
| **Tier 2 — Growth** | Rocket gently lifts off with a subtle flame trail 🚀 (slow, steady rise) |
| **Tier 3.A — Skilled** | Rocket launches faster with a longer flame trail and slight shake (mid-speed, energetic) |
| **Tier 3.B — Leadership** | Rocket blasts off with a bright glow, particle-like sparks, and a trail that spans the full card height (fastest, most dramatic) |

### Visual Details
- **Rocket icon**: Lucide `Rocket` icon with accent color glow
- **Flame trail**: A gradient line/glow behind the rocket that fades out, using the brand amber color
- **Motion**: Continuous subtle floating animation (up/down bobbing) after initial launch
- **Position**: Top-left or side of each card, flying upward along the card edge
- **No clutter**: Keeps the animation subtle and professional — enhances the card without distracting from pricing info

### What stays the same
- Card layout, pricing info, features, hover video preview — all unchanged
- Only adds the rocket animation as a decorative enhancement
