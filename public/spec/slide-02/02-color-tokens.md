## Roles
- Primary text: `--pres-text`
- Supporting copy: `--pres-text-muted`
- Highlighted problem/metric: `--pres-accent`; outage problem may use `--destructive`
- Readability overlay: `--pres-bg` from 98% opacity to 8%
