# Issue 001 — Never Show Combined Price + Bonus Totals

## Problem
Showing "Price + Bonus → Total" (e.g., $1,900 + $5,000 → $6,900/mo) makes customers think they need to pay the total amount. The bonus is FREE but the equation format creates price confusion.

## Rule
**NEVER** display a combined total of price + bonus. The bonus is a gift, not an additional cost.

## Correct Pattern
- Show "You pay: $1,900/mo → You get: $5,000/mo FREE"
- Use green color + Gift icon + "FREE" label on the bonus
- Separate the two amounts visually — no math equations

## Wrong Pattern
- ❌ $1,900 + $5,000 = $6,900/mo
- ❌ "Total value: $6,900"
- ❌ Any combined number that looks like a price

## Applied To
- Slide 05 (PremiumExpertSlideV2) — tier bonus section
