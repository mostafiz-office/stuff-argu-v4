# Issue 003 — Bonus Panel Must Be High-Visibility

## Problem
The bonus section heading and tier rows were too small and dense, making them hard to read at presentation scale.

## Rule
- Heading: Ubuntu font, ≥22px, bold, with icon-box (gradient background)
- Tier names: Ubuntu font, ≥17px, bold
- Prices: Poppins, ≥18px, semibold
- FREE badges: Ubuntu, ≥17px, bold, green with background pill
- Row padding: ≥14px vertical
- Row gap: ≥3 (Tailwind gap-3)

## Why
Presentations are viewed at a distance or scaled down. Small/dense text kills scannability. Every element in the bonus panel must be instantly readable.
